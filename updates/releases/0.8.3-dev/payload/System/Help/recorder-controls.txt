Enter starts recording. Left/Right changes microphone gain from 1x to 4096x. Enter any whole-number gain in Microphone gain. More gain makes quiet speech louder; lower it when Clipping appears. Gain is saved after capture stops. Space pauses/resumes. Enter or Esc stops and saves. Fn+apostrophe or G0 saves and closes. The microphone and single level bar use your wallpaper colors. Files are standard 16 kHz mono WAV in Recordings. Interrupted .wav.part files remain recoverable. Playback and recording are exclusive.
